Give your AI a world to understand

Grove builds a live, time-aware map of your world from the sources you bring. Your AI host reads it and stores in it, with your consent.

Sign in How Grove handles your data

What Grove is

  • A map of your worldGrove keeps typed items, such as people, plans, events and decisions, and the links between them. You see them as one map.
  • It knows about timeGrove records when each claim was last confirmed and what kind of source said it.
  • Your AI host works from itWith your consent, an AI host reads your world and stores in it through a scoped API. It sees what the scope allows.

What you can bring

  • AI conversationsExports from your AI tools, such as ChatGPT and Claude.
  • ChatsWhatsApp chat exports.
  • Notes and filesNotes, documents and screenshots.
  • Voice notesAudio that Grove has transcribed.
  • CalendarGoogle Calendar, read-only, in its own consent.

The privacy page marks each data path as Runs or Planned, so you can see what works today.

Why Grove asks Google for your name and address

You can sign in with Google or with a code sent to your email address. With Google, Grove asks for your name and your address, and nothing else: the address finds your world, and the name goes on your own item in it.

The calendar is a separate request. Grove asks to read your calendar only when you connect it, in its own consent screen, and it never asks at sign-in.

Who runs it

Grove is run by X-ARC. To ask a question or to have your world deleted, write to contact@x-arc.ai. The privacy page says what Grove reads, where your data goes and how to forget.
