What Grove reads

At sign-in, Grove reads what Google sends (your name, your address and a flag that says Google verified the address), or the address you type to get an email code.

After that, Grove reads only what you bring or connect: chats, notes, files and images you upload, voice notes, and your calendar if you connect it. The table under Where your data goes says which of these paths run today.

Grove does not ask Google for your mail or your files.

What Grove asks Google for, and why

Google sign-in asks for three scopes, and no others. The calendar scope is a separate request that you see only if you connect the calendar.

ScopeWhy Grove asks
openidAt sign-in, to know it is you.
emailAt sign-in, for your address, which finds your world.
profileAt sign-in, for your name, which goes on your own item.
calendar.readonlyOnly when you connect the calendar, in its own consent. Read-only, to learn your events.

Where your data goes

Each row is one path your data can take. Runs means the path is live today. Planned means it does not run yet, and no data of yours takes it.

Where it is stored

Grove's store runs on a server of X-ARC at Hetzner in Helsinki, Finland. A daily offline dump is kept on the same server for 30 days.

Grove keeps hashes, not the values, for your address, for session tokens and for sign-in codes. Your name from Google is stored as the title of your own item.

Which models see it

Sign-in uses no model. When the paths in the table run, these models see the data the table states:

  • Meta Model API, Muse Spark 1.3: screened text.
  • Meta Model API, Voice Transcribe: voice notes.
  • Muse Glimmer on Modal: screened text and images. Until Modal's data terms are named here, this path carries synthetic data only.

When the upload path runs, screening in code on Grove's server comes first. The terms of each path are in the table.

How to forget

The forget control is not built yet. It comes with a later release, and this section will then say how to use it.

Today, write to contact@x-arc.ai to have your world deleted. A dump made before the deletion stays on the server until its 30 days end.

Logging out does not delete your world. It ends the session, and your next sign-in opens the same world.

Cookies

Grove sets two cookies. It has no tracking and no analytics.

CookieWhat it does
grove_sessionKeeps you signed in, for up to 30 days. Grove stores only a hash of its value.
__Host-grove_googleThe short Google state cookie. It lives for 10 minutes while you sign in with Google, to check that Google's answer belongs to your request.

The map also keeps your view preferences, your onboarding steps and the address you typed in your browser's local storage. They do not leave your browser.
