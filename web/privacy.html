What Grove reads

At sign-in, Grove reads what Google sends (your name, your address and a flag that says Google verified the address), or the address you type to get an email code.

After that, Grove reads only what you bring or connect: chats, notes, files and images you upload, voice notes, and your calendar if you connect it. The table under Where your data goes distinguishes available code, configuration and pending work.

The direct Git connector reads commit metadata from a local clone approved for your world. It does not fetch remote changes, run hooks or read worktree files. A commit does not prove deployment.

Pipedream connections use a separate hosted authorization page and account-status checks. Grove sends an opaque owner identifier and the selected app to Pipedream. This component imports no source content and runs no provider actions. A connection status is not an import result.

Grove's Google sign-in and direct Calendar connector do not request Gmail or Drive access. Pipedream requests provider consent separately; review its scopes on the hosted page.

Grove's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What Grove asks Google for, and why

Google sign-in asks for three scopes, and no others. The calendar scope is a separate request that you see only if you connect the calendar.

ScopeWhy Grove asks
openidAt sign-in, to know it is you. At calendar consent, to identify the Google account you connect.
emailAt sign-in, for your address, which finds your world.
profileAt sign-in, for your name, which goes on your own item.
calendar.readonlyOnly when you connect the calendar, in its own consent. Read-only, to learn your events.

Grove follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, for Google API data.

Where it is stored

The deployment configuration places Grove's store on an X-ARC server at Hetzner in Helsinki, Finland. The backup policy specifies a daily offline dump on the same server, kept for 30 days. This page does not verify that a backup completed.

Grove keeps hashes, not the values, for your address, for session tokens and for sign-in codes. Your name from Google is stored as the title of your own item.

Which models see it

Sign-in uses no model. When the paths in the table run, these models see the data the table states:

  • Meta Model API, Muse Spark 1.3: screened text.
  • Meta Model API, Voice Transcribe: voice notes.
  • Muse Glimmer on Modal: screened text and images. This build run uses synthetic data only.

When the upload path runs, screening in code on Grove's server comes first. There is no local runner. Ambiguous sensitive content is dropped or masked; it is not sent to a model for screening.

Provider policies: Meta Model API Terms of Service, Modal Terms of Service, Modal Privacy Policy, Resend Privacy Policy, and Resend Data Processing Addendum.

How to forget

Open the forget control, sign in, choose an item, a field or an exact phrase, then select Review removal. Check the selection and select Confirm and forget. Each request requires a new approval.

The control refuses a request when the private forget ledger is not configured. A successful receipt lists counts and the date when older backups expire. The receipt does not repeat the removed content.

Forget removes matching content from Grove's store, history and indexes. A restore must replay the latest separate ledger before it serves data. Copies already downloaded or sent to an AI provider are outside this operation.

If the control is unavailable, write to contact@x-arc.ai. An AI host needs the sensitive-write scope and your separate approval for every forget request.

Logging out does not delete your world. It ends the session, and your next sign-in opens the same world.

Cookies

Grove uses three cookies. It has no tracking and no analytics.

CookieWhat it does
grove_sessionKeeps you signed in, for up to 30 days. Grove stores only a hash of its value.
__Host-grove_googleThe short Google state cookie. It lives for 10 minutes while you sign in with Google, to check that Google's answer belongs to your request.
__Host-grove_calendarThe encrypted calendar consent state. It lives for 10 minutes and binds Google's answer to your current Grove session.

The map also keeps your view preferences, your onboarding steps and the address you typed in your browser's local storage. They do not leave your browser.
